Sheet 03: the EDGE layout as it stands, beside IQON West.
EDGE projectPlots 01–107 with the club house, on Ac 13 – 10 Gts. Roads are 30 ft and 40 ft wide. Amenities and utilities blocks sit on the east edge.
IQON West projectThe neighbouring layout to the east, labelled Phase-1 layout on sheet 05. It runs on 30 ft and 40 ft roads and fronts the 30 m existing / 90 m proposed road. Two pockets of Ankura balance land sit at its south end.
NalaA natural drain runs south-west to north-east between EDGE and IQON West. A 9 m buffer on each side doubles as tot-lot green.
Teegla Vaagu & canalThe stream on IQON West's north edge, with the canal along its east edge.
Entry from the westA 12 m road comes up from the Chevella–Sangareddy existing road. It crosses the nala on a culvert into EDGE.
Site-B stripPlots 01–04 sit in a row along the nala, between EDGE and IQON West, next to the green zone.
